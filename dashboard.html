A-Z SMM Setup Guide

Phase 1: Render Deployment (Free)

Go to your Render Dashboard, select your Would-final repository, and set the Runtime to Docker. Use the Free tier instance and deploy.

Phase 2: Telegram Bots & Chat IDs

Open Telegram and message @BotFather. Create a new bot using /newbot. Add your bot as Admin in your group. Get your group ID using @raw_data_bot. Paste these under individual Personas settings to separate groups!

Phase 3: Free Google Gemini AI Key

Navigate to Google AI Studio. Click "Get API Key" and project-register a 100% free Gemini API key (supports 15 free requests/min). Paste this inside your AI Settings section!

Phase 4: iOS Mobile Cookies bookmarklet

Since iPhones have no F12 keys, create a bookmarked URL in Safari named "Get Cookies" and paste this code as its URL:

javascript:(function(){var c=document.cookie; if(!c){alert('No cookies found. Log in!');}else{prompt('Copy your cookies below:', c);}})();

Go to Twitter/TikTok/Instagram in Safari, log in, tap your bookmarklet, and copy your session keys (e.g. auth_token or sessionid) into the SMM Fleet forms sequentially to connect unlimited accounts!
